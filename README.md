# 📚 StudyHub

### Your academic resources. Organized by semester. Built for students.

**StudyHub** is a student-focused academic resource platform that brings lecture slides, notes, past papers, tutorials, assignments, deadlines, and Kuppi sessions into one organized place.

Instead of searching through scattered WhatsApp messages, Telegram groups, Google Drive folders, or old files, students can find the resources they need **by semester and subject — without creating an account.**

🌐 **Website:** https://learn-trical.vercel.app/

🤖 **Telegram Bot:** https://t.me/StudyGeniusX_bot

---

## ✨ Why StudyHub?

University resources are often spread across multiple platforms, making it difficult to find the right material at the right time.

StudyHub provides a simple academic workspace where students can:

* 📖 Browse resources by semester and subject
* 📑 Access lecture slides, notes, and past papers
* 📝 Find tutorials and assignments
* ⏰ Track upcoming deadlines
* 🎓 Discover Kuppi sessions and presenters
* 🔎 Search materials quickly
* 📥 Preview and download PDFs
* 🤖 Open materials with ChatGPT or Gemini for studying
* 📱 Access materials through Telegram
* 🔔 Receive deadline reminders
* 💬 Report missing or broken resources
* 📲 Install StudyHub as a Progressive Web App where supported

> **One calm place for every lecture slide, past paper and deadline.**

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

Visit the StudyHub website:

**https://learn-trical.vercel.app/**

No account or sign-up is required for students.

### 1. Choose Your Semester

Open **Dashboard** and select the semester you are currently studying.

### 2. Select Your Subjects

Go to **Preferences** and select all the subjects you are taking.

Your preferences are stored locally on your current device, allowing StudyHub to personalize your dashboard without requiring an account.

### 3. Explore Your Dashboard

Your dashboard brings together relevant:

* 📚 Lecture slides
* 📝 Notes
* 📄 Past papers
* 📌 Tutorials
* 📋 Assignments
* ⏰ Deadlines
* 🎓 Kuppi sessions

### 4. Study the Material

Open any material to:

* Preview the resource
* Download the PDF
* Open it on the website
* Send it to ChatGPT
* Send it to Gemini

# 🛡️ Administration

StudyHub contains two protected administration areas.

## Semester Admin

Semester administrators can manage resources associated with their assigned semester, including:

* Materials
* Deadlines
* Kuppi sessions
* Modules
* Requests
* Profile information
* Feedback

---

## Super Admin

Super administrators have broader platform-level management capabilities.

They can manage:

* Semesters
* Subjects
* Modules
* Users
* Materials
* Deadlines
* Kuppi sessions
* Broadcasts
* Notifications
* Analytics
* AI settings
* Pending changes

### Approval Workflow

Changes requested by semester administrators can be placed into the super-admin approval queue.

```text
Semester Admin
      │
      ▼
Request Change
      │
      ▼
Pending Approval
      │
      ▼
Super Admin Review
      │
   ┌──┴──┐
   ▼     ▼
Approve Reject
   │
   ▼
Published Change
```

This provides an additional layer of control over important content changes.

# 🤝 Contributing

Contributions that improve StudyHub are welcome.

When contributing:

### Follow the project structure

Routes are file-based under:

```text
src/routes/
```

Do **not** manually edit:

```text
src/routeTree.gen.ts
```

---

### Keep student-facing content safe

Student-facing reads should only expose:

```text
Published
+
Non-pending
content
```

---

### Protect administrative operations

Admin and super-admin operations should use the existing:

* Supabase authentication helpers
* Role checks
* Authorization mechanisms

---

### Keep secrets secure

Never commit:

```text
.env
```

or other files containing credentials, API keys, tokens, or deployment secrets.

---

### Update documentation

The `/help` route should be treated as the source of truth when updating student-facing workflow documentation.
